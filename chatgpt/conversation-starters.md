# Conversation Starters

Concise, reusable prompts for the Career Targeting Intelligence Custom
GPT's Conversation Starters field. Free of real user data.

- "Here is my resume. Build my Candidate Profile."
- "Turn my preferences into approved Search Criteria."
- "Find product companies that match my profile and commute limit."
- "Rank these companies and explain the exclusions."
- "Find relevant recruiters and engineering managers at these companies."
- "Verify who posted a relevant job during the last 90 days."
- "Build a manual Outreach Queue from these verified results."
- "Continue from this Research State without rebuilding approved work."
