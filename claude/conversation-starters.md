# Conversation Starters

Short, platform-neutral prompts that demonstrate the range of the Career
Targeting Intelligence Project — from a first-time candidate analysis to a
narrow, focused follow-up. Suitable for a Project's conversation-starter
field or an onboarding message.

- "Here is my resume. Build my candidate profile."
- "Find 30 product companies within my commute limit."
- "Find recruiters at the top five companies."
- "Find engineering managers relevant to my background."
- "Verify who posted relevant jobs during the last 90 days."
- "Explain why this company is Priority 2."
- "Build my outreach queue."
- "Continue from this Research State file."
